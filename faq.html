How it works

Archer runs directly on your TradingView chart in three stages. Nothing here places a trade — it marks levels and tracks price against them.

01
Scanning

Momentum scan

Archer reads price and momentum continuously, looking for the specific shift it's tuned to detect. No level is shown yet — this is the resting state most of the time.

02
Waiting

Level published

When the shift is detected, Archer draws a price level before price has reached it. The chart marks this state as Waiting — the level is published, but nothing has triggered.

03
Confirmed

Stop, targets & live ticks

If price actually trades through the level, it's marked Confirmed and Archer draws a volatility-scaled stop (1.5x ATR by default) plus five targets above it. Each target gets a live ✓ the instant price touches it — updated on the touch, not at candle close.

Frequently asked questions

Does Archer place trades for me?

No. Archer is a chart analysis tool. It plots levels on your TradingView chart and can fire alerts, but it places no orders and connects to no broker. Every entry, exit and sizing decision is made by you.

How does Archer analyze the market?

Archer continuously reads momentum and volatility on your chart in real time, then plots a level, an ATR-scaled stop and a five-target ladder as price action develops — no manual chart-reading required. It doesn't guarantee an outcome; it marks a level and tracks price against it live.

What markets and timeframes does Archer support?

Futures (NQ/MNQ, ES/MES, YM/MYM, RTY/M2K, GC/MGC, SI/SIL, CL/MCL), crypto (BTC, ETH), and major stocks (SPY, QQQ, AAPL, NVDA, TSLA and others). It runs on any timeframe — defaults ship tuned for intraday charts, and stop multiple, hold time and expiry are all exposed in settings.

Does Archer run overnight and on weekends?

On futures, Archer stands down between 4pm and 6pm New York and across the weekend. The thinnest-liquidity hours of the session are deliberately excluded.

How do I get access?

Subscribe through Whop and enter your TradingView username — access to the invite-only script is granted automatically through Whop's TradingView integration.

Why is Archer invite-only instead of public on TradingView?

Invite-only is how TradingView gates a protected script to paying subscribers only. It also ties access directly to an active Whop subscription, and removes it automatically if that subscription lapses.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. The subscription runs monthly through Whop and can be cancelled any time through Whop's billing.

Does Archer set up alerts?

Yes — Archer ships with three TradingView alert conditions, so you can get notified without watching the chart continuously.

Can I see Archer running before I subscribe?

Yes — Archer runs on a public live stream on YouTube, plotting levels on a real chart in real time.
